When the Internet feels slow or keeps dropping, the first question is where the fault is: this device's Wi-Fi, the router, or the Internet provider. Ambysto Steady measures all three while it is open and tells you which one it is, in plain words.

LIVE MEASUREMENTS
• Latency, packet loss and jitter to your router and to two public Internet servers, every second
• Real connections (TCP) alongside ping, so a provider that merely slows down ping is not mistaken for an outage
• Connection type, IPv4/IPv6, DNS, metered network and Low Data Mode at a glance

CHECKS THAT EXPLAIN THE CAUSE
• Ping quality over the last hour: a local Wi-Fi problem, a provider problem, or nothing to fix
• DNS benchmark: is the DNS server in use slow or failing, compared with well-known public ones?
• VPN detection: a VPN changes routing and DNS, so results are read with that in mind
• Bufferbloat test, on request: does latency explode when the line is busy?
• On the Mac, also: Wi-Fi signal, channel interference from nearby networks, and how often the link collapses
Each result comes with advice on what to try first.

HISTORY
Latency and loss over the last hour, day or week, from the measurements kept on your device.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no tracking, no analytics, no ads
• Measurements stay on your device for 30 days and are never sent anywhere; you can delete them at any time
• The app does not read your Wi-Fi network's name or address
• To report a problem you see the whole report first and send it yourself

Good to know: iPhone and iPad do not let apps run measurements in the background, so Ambysto Steady measures while it is open. It finds the cause of a problem; it cannot make your provider faster.
